One brain.A single healthy adult, scanned 27 times and averaged for a clean picture, then aligned to MNI space, the shared map neuroscientists use.

Many ways to see it.Each method passes a different kind of energy through the head and measures what comes out.

MRIHydrogen nuclei in water and fat show soft tissue in fine detail.

CTX-rays show bone and fresh blood within seconds.

PETA radioactive glucose tracer shows which regions use the most energy.

Diffusion MRIWater moving along nerve fibres shows the direction of white-matter tracts.

fMRIOxygen-rich blood reaching active cortex shows where the brain is working.
